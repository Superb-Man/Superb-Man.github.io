I graduated from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) with a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering. I always enjoy exploring how things work, especially when there's something interesting to figure out or an idea worth experimenting with. That curiosity eventually led me toward computer systems, an area I hope to explore and contribute further through research.

After graduating from BUET, I joined Samsung Research Bangladesh as a Research and Development Engineer. My work involves more than just developing software; before a solution makes its way into a product, we often need to investigate the underlying problem, explore possible approaches, and demonstrate that our proposed solution actually works. I've enjoyed this process of experimentation and validation, along with the practical challenges of turning ideas into reliable software. Outside work, I enjoy drawing, playing football, watching tennis, and reading about a variety of topics, both technical and non-technical.

I want to pursue a PhD in Fall 2027 to deepen my research interests in computer systems, identify critical challenges and problems, explore new ideas and possible solutions in depth, and contribute to the field through meaningful research. This website is a collection of my projects, experiences, writings, and a few things I find interesting along the way.
