Experimental Projects

Logless replication and recovery · Oct 2025 – Present

disKeyV

An ongoing experimental logless distributed key–value store built around segment-based replication and recovery. It uses TCP communication, heartbeat-based failure detection and leader transitions, with crash recovery and safe-read handling. Further work considers software-emulated RDMA-style transport and recovery correctness.
