01 / ACADEMIC WORK

Research

Undergraduate thesis · Manuscript under review

Patient Similarity Detection via Contrastive Learning

Undergraduate thesis on patient similarity detection using physiological signals from the Stanford Wearable PPG and PTB Diagnostic ECG datasets. Developed a novel contrastive loss function tailored to patient similarity learning and adapted ResNeXt and EfficientNet architectures for 1D time-series representation learning. Designed and optimized a K-nearest neighbors (KNN)-based inference pipeline for similarity search and label prediction over large patient databases, incorporating parallel processing to improve inference efficiency and reduce latency. Evaluated the proposed approach against existing baselines. Manuscript submitted to a Q1 journal (under review).
